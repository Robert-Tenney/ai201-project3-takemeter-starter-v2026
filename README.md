## Label Taxonomy

Scope: text in r/touhou. Examples below come from Help, Fan Discussion and
Game Discussion posts, and from top-level comments in the Fan Discussion
thread "What touhou opinion would put you in this situation?". Excluded:
OC: Art, Found Fanart, Meme, Video and Cosplay posts (no text), fanfiction
chapters, mod and weekly threads, and ads.

### `analysis`

**Definition:** Makes a point backed by a specific, checkable thing from the
franchise (a game, character, spell card, date, or quote), and the point
depends on it.

**Example 1 (comment):**
> It's less a hard canon fact and a bit more of extrapolation, but Koishi has
> what feels like the biggest evidence of that Spell Card rules aren't as
> foolproof as the community likes to paint them and that you probably can
> loophole abuse.
>
> Release of Id and Superego are very similar in structure and it's extra
> notorious because they're almost back to back, only separated by a
> non-spell. They shoot heart-shaped danmaku in sort of constantly rotating
> parabolic trajectories with the individual lanes criss-crossing. The main
> difference being that former is shot outwards (and gameplay-wise has hearts
> pile up at the bottom of screen), while the latter "falls" inwards (and has
> the hearts disappear when they reach their target). Even Twilight Frontier
> seems to have noticed; when she's playable in the air fighters they're
> still separate supers, but her story appearance in Hopeless Masquerade just
> merges them into one Spell Card that alternates between the two patterns.
>
> Ref, give her a yellow card, girl put more effort into re-using the
> structure of Rorschach in Danmaku for Genetics of the Unconscious than
> whatever the hell is happening here. There's even a break with dick danmaku
> Embers of Love in-between.
>
> It also doesn't really help that some of Marisa's comments in Grimoire of
> Marisa boil down to something like "yeah good luck dealing with this one".

**Example 2 (comment):**
> No, we don't need Mima back; there are several characters who fulfill that
> role in the current canon, including Mizuchi.
>
> Continuing with the topic of Mizuchi, she, along with the three fairies,
> would be among the best choices for fighting games.
>
> Yorihime did win fairly in Silent Sinner in Blue. Furthermore, that same
> manga confirms that the moon rabbits lead normal and relatively happy
> lives, so she's not an abusive slave driver either.

### `hot_take`

**Definition:** A confident claim about Touhou with no support offered, or
with a name or fact that is only decoration.

**Example 1 (comment):**
> The undeniable fact that sakuya sucks

**Example 2 (comment):**
> Fumos are over hyped and many jokes in the fandom suck

### `experience`

**Definition:** Describes the writer's own situation or feelings with Touhou,
without asking for anything or making a claim about the franchise.

**Example 1 (comment):**
> For me EoSD and LoLK are by far my favorite, I'm just biased though.
> Actually I like PCB and IN a lot. PoFV I totally get it. Is it not fair to
> give them a pass for being older?

**Example 2 (comment):**
> I'm just a Reimu(all),Sakuya(fanon) and Yukari(fanon) hater and a
> Yuuka,letty,Seiga lover. that's far from what you're asking.

### The hardest boundary

**Which two labels:** `analysis` vs `hot_take`

**The post that sits between them (comment):**
> Every Touhou game from 10 to 15 is superior in gameplay, characters, story
> and music to every game from Touhou 6 to 9. With EoSD being by far the most
> extremely overrated game in the series and IN being the only one of the
> older windows games that still holds up in most aspects.

**The decision rule I used every time:** 
Naming a game is not support. If the point would fall apart without a
specific fact the writer actually cites (a spell card's structure, a manga
event), it's `analysis`. If the names are only labels on a judgment, it's
`hot_take`, even if the tone is calm.

**Second boundary:** `experience` vs `hot_take`, from "Junko is easier than
Clownpiece. I can do Junko in 2 lives with Reisen, Clown took me 4." The
claim rests only on the writer's own runs, so it's a feeling and a claim at
once.
